About
STEP Viewer v3.3

Browser-based STEP viewer for mechanical design reviews — CAD-style navigation, sectioning and inspection tools. Runs fully local: models never leave this machine.

Change Log
v3.3 July 2026
Selection
  • Click a part in the 3D view — it highlights blue and its row lights up in the feature tree (collapsed branches expand and the row scrolls into view).
  • Click a name in the feature tree — that part (or the whole sub-assembly) highlights blue in the 3D view.
  • Click the same row / empty space, or press Escape, to deselect. Original colors are always restored exactly.
Compare
  • Compare button (next to Open) loads a second model on top of the first — both models show in the tree, every part can be hidden, and all tools work across both.
  • Repaint colors model 1 blue and model 2 red so revision differences pop; Reset Colors restores the exact original colors (including per-face colors).
  • Transparent Compare — a slider fades geometry that exists in both models; unique volume stays solid, so changes stand out. A Swap button inverts which bodies fade (shared ⇄ unique).
  • Transparent Compare also works with a single model — it fades bodies that occupy the same space inside one assembly (duplicate or stacked parts, e.g. double-inserted components).
  • Image-quality switches re-tessellate all compared models together.
Interface
  • Measure, Section and the other floating panels can be dragged anywhere by their header, or pinned next to the feature tree — layout is remembered.
  • Selection filter moved to the bottom-right corner and is collapsible — a gold dot on the collapsed pill marks an active filter.
  • Zoom to Fit keeps the current orientation — it only zooms, never re-orients the model (initial load still opens in the standard iso view).
  • All floating panels (Section, Measure, Transparent Compare, …) can collapse to just their header bar (▾ in the header); state is remembered.
  • Orthographic / Perspective merged into one switch button that shows the current projection.
  • Transparent Compare slider is now 0–100% with fine control near the ends and faster change through the middle.
  • SPT-ME logo can be hidden from here (checkbox below) for external-use screenshots.
v3.2 July 2026
  • Geometry engine patched to a 4 GB heap (up from 2 GB) — dense ~77 MB+ assemblies that used to run out of memory now load in-browser
  • Open .glb/.gltf meshes and STEP assemblies up to 500 MB (convert once with tools/step2glb.py); auto-Low + minimal-detail rescue for large files
v3.0 July 2026
Navigation
  • Creo-style trackball — free unlimited rotation, Creo mouse buttons (middle spin, Shift+middle pan, Ctrl+middle zoom), zoom to cursor.
  • One shared rotation center for mouse and 3D mouse, marked by an RGB-axis pivot glyph.
  • 3Dconnexion SpaceMouse support through the 3DxWare driver — always on, auto-reconnect.
  • ViewCube: click faces, edges or corners (visible dots, hover highlight) to orient; faces colored by axis; roll / tip arrows and home.
  • X/Y/Z orientation triad at bottom-left, colors matched to the ViewCube.
  • Views menu: Isometric / Dimetric / Trimetric, named views, previous view, zoom window; Normal To a face (Ctrl+8).
  • Orthographic projection by default.
Section
  • Drafting-standard hatched caps — unique angle per part, ASME / ISO presets, zoom-stable spacing, 2:1 heavy cut outlines, color options.
  • Start a section from any picked flat face (Face button).
  • Picking a cylinder or cone sections through its centerline, aligned to a selectable X/Y/Z plane.
Display
  • Shadows menu (Inventor-style): ground shadow, object shadows, ambient occlusion.
  • Redesigned toolbar pictograms — the three display styles share one cube icon.
  • Background toggle hides backdrop and grid together; off by default.
  • Mass properties, graphics preferences, hidden-lines and tangent-edge display styles.
Loading
  • STEP parsing runs in a background worker — the UI stays responsive.
  • Progress bar with time estimate; parts appear in the scene while the model builds.
  • Progressive load: mid-size STEP files (3–40 MB) show a fast coarse preview first, then refine to the chosen quality in place with the camera preserved — final quality is unchanged. Toggle in Graphics Preferences.
  • Out-of-memory on very large files is detected, retried at Low quality and reported clearly.
General
  • Toolbar regrouped — Zoom to Fit and Section moved to View.
  • Portable single-file build: STEP-Viewer.html.
v1.0 baseline
  • STEP import (Open CASCADE), feature tree, display styles, measure, exploded view, selection filters.

Built with three.js, occt-import-js (Open CASCADE) and 3DconnexionJS.
